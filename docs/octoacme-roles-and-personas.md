# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers and User Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers understand user needs and shape usable, accessible experiences. They may contribute during discovery, design, and delivery, depending on the project.

### Responsibilities
- Plan or conduct user research and synthesize findings
- Create user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Validate designs with users and incorporate feedback
- Identify usability and accessibility considerations
- Communicate design intent and constraints to the delivery team

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to connect user needs and research findings to product goals and priorities.
- Work with Developers to assess feasibility, explain design intent, and refine solutions during implementation.
- Coordinate with Project Managers on research and design activities, dependencies, and milestone timing.
- Share research findings and design decisions with stakeholders to inform planning and acceptance criteria.

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify business and stakeholder needs and translate them into actionable requirements. This role is especially useful when workflows, rules, or cross-team requirements need detailed analysis.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document stakeholder needs, workflows, and business rules
- Break needs into clear requirements and acceptance criteria
- Identify gaps, assumptions, and dependencies in proposed requirements
- Maintain traceability between needs, backlog items, and validation
- Support stakeholders and delivery teams in resolving requirement questions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to ensure requirements support product outcomes and priorities; the Product Manager retains product prioritization decisions.
- Coordinate with Project Managers to sequence analysis, track dependencies, and surface risks.
- Collaborate with Developers to clarify requirements and explore implementation trade-offs.
- Partner with QA/Testing to make requirements testable and ensure acceptance criteria can be verified.

---

## Technical Leads and Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction and help ensure that solutions fit the system architecture and engineering standards. The role may be shared or scaled to the project's complexity.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design, architecture, and integration decisions
- Identify technical risks, constraints, and dependencies
- Support engineering estimates and decomposition of technical work
- Encourage maintainability, reliability, security, and appropriate testing
- Share technical decisions and rationale with the team

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Support Developers through technical guidance, design discussions, and implementation coordination.
- Partner with Product Managers to explain technical options and trade-offs that affect product outcomes.
- Work with Project Managers to surface technical dependencies, risks, and schedule impacts.
- Coordinate with QA/Testing and Support/Operations on testability, observability, and operational readiness.
- Keep decision ownership clear: technical guidance informs trade-offs, while product and project priorities remain with their respective owners.

---

## Delivery or Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Delivery or Engineering Managers support the team's ability to deliver sustainably by helping address capacity, team health, and impediments. Depending on the organization, these responsibilities may be part of another role.

### Responsibilities
- Support team capacity planning and sustainable ways of working
- Identify and help remove team-level impediments
- Monitor delivery health and raise concerns about execution risks
- Support team development, collaboration, and continuous improvement
- Coordinate engineering resourcing or people-management needs where applicable

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with Project Managers on delivery plans, capacity constraints, and impediments; the Project Manager remains responsible for project coordination and status.
- Partner with Technical Leads on engineering execution and technical-team needs.
- Work with Product Managers to communicate delivery trade-offs that may affect scope or timing.
- Collaborate with Developers to improve team practices and address barriers to effective work.

---

## Support, Operations, and Site Reliability Engineers

### Role Summary
Support, Operations, and Site Reliability Engineers help ensure services can be operated, monitored, and supported after release. Their involvement is especially valuable for production changes or systems with significant reliability needs.

### Responsibilities
- Define operational readiness needs, including monitoring, alerting, and runbooks
- Review reliability, capacity, and support considerations for planned changes
- Prepare support handoffs and help teams understand incident procedures
- Participate in production incident response and follow-up improvements
- Identify operational risks before release and recommend mitigations

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers to incorporate operability, monitoring, and reliability into implementation.
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on production-like validation and release checks.
- Partner with Project Managers and release owners on readiness, dependencies, and deployment coordination.
- Work with Product Managers to communicate service impact and operational trade-offs.
- Coordinate with Developers and Project Managers during incidents and contribute operational findings to retrospectives.

---

## Applying These Roles

These are candidate personas, not required positions for every project. Choose roles based on project scope and risk; one person may cover multiple roles. Make decision ownership, handoffs, and escalation paths explicit for each project.
